## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: a side notice sets its icon on the first line

In the board look the icon of a detail page's side notice MUST align to the start of the notice (beside the first line of text), not to its centre.

#### Scenario: DqZaak "Advies gevraagd"
- GIVEN the side notice "Advies gevraagd aan Mobiliteit en verkeer..." over three lines
- THEN the warning icon stands beside the first line
