## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A refused board option keeps the dark edge on the sunken ground

In the board look a refused (disabled) choice option SHALL be edged in the dark border grey and sit on the board's
sunken ground.

#### Scenario: Next step dialog
- **GIVEN** the Next step dialog on a case that is not suspended
- **THEN** "Hervatten" is edged in the dark border grey on the sunken ground
