# grouped-cards Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Grouped cards sit in a titled card

On a detail page card surface a `grouped-cards` widget SHALL be drawn inside the host's titled card with its manifest
title and subtitle, and in the board look a card with actions SHALL hold them at its end beside its title and meta.

#### Scenario: DqZaaktype Fasen en taken

- **THEN** "Fasen en taken" and "Elke taak heeft een eigen rol, termijn en formulier ..." head one card holding the phases
- **AND** each task's "···" sits at the end of its row
