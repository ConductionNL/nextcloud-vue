## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board header link under the title sits 10px under it

In the board look, a `CnWidgetWrapper` header link placed under the title on a dashboard SHALL sit 10px under the title
at the font's normal line height.

#### Scenario: DqDashboard "Vastgelopen zaken"

- **THEN** "Alles bekijken" starts 10px under the title and the first row about 54px under the title's top
