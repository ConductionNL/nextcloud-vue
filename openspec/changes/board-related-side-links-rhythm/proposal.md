# Proposal: board-related-side-links-rhythm

## Why

A related-objects card in a board side column that lists its relations as links (`layout: "links"`, PqKassabon,
PqKassadienst and PqLead "Gerelateerd") drew them at the body's 15px and 1.5 leading, 8px apart: 31px from link to link
and the first link 2px under the title. The boards draw side links at 14px, normal leading, 10px apart, the first 10px
under the title, as dossiq's side links (DqZaak "Gekoppeld") read.

## What changes

Board look, side column only: the links take 14px at the normal leading and a 10px gap, and a card that opens on its
links starts them 10px under the title.

## Reach

Board look, a related-objects card in a detail page's side column with link rows. The sections and tab layouts do not
move.
