**Status**: proposed
**Scope**: nextcloud-vue

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board banner actions are drawn alike

In the board look a banner action, link or button, SHALL be the board control height and SHALL have no outer margin, so the actions stand at the banner gap apart.

#### Scenario: DqMijnWerk Vandaag eerst

- **THEN** the four actions are 40px high and 10px apart
