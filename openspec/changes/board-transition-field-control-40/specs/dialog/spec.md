## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board dialog's transition field control is 40px high

In a board dialog, a transition input field SHALL draw its control at the board's field height (40px), with no wrapper
padding above or below it.

#### Scenario: Opschorten in the lifecycle dialog
- **GIVEN** the case lifecycle dialog in the board look, Opschorten chosen
- **THEN** the Reden, Dagen and terug-op controls are 40px high
