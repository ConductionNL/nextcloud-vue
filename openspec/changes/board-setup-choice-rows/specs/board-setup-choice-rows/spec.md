# board-setup-choice-rows Delta

**Status**: proposed
**Scope**: nextcloud-vue

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A setup step's choices are drawn as the board

In the board look a setup step's chosen option SHALL have a 2px border and no inset shadow with its content in place,
and the step's options SHALL draw no icon.

#### Scenario: DqInstellen "Voorbeeldgegevens laden"

- **THEN** the chosen row measures a 2px primary border and no box shadow, and neither row shows an icon
