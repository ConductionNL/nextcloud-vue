# A data card's label and value on the board's rhythm

## Why

The boards draw a fact as dt over dd, 3px apart, on the normal line height (194 of 212 pairs across the Dq boards). Live sets the label at 13px/19.5 and the value at 15px/22.5 with a 2px gap, so every data card runs taller and its first label sits lower (DqZaakContact Ontvangstbevestiging r29: 33.8 under the title vs 29).
