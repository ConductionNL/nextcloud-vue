# Board look for the links page

## Why
pipelinq's Geavanceerd hub (CnLinkCardsPage) had no board-look skin. pl2 PqGeavanceerdHub: cards in 274px columns instead of 362, the dark control border instead of the hairline, status pills 25.5px tall instead of 21 in the main ink, no page heading (the title was an h2 behind the navigation-toggle gap), icons without their tile and no trailing chevron.

## What changes
- The page title is the page's h1 and the group captions are h2.
- A grid card ends with a chevron, or an open-in-new mark when it opens a new tab, as a list row already does.
- In the board look the page takes the board's padding, header, captions, 320px columns 16px apart, card border, padding and icon tile, label and description type, a 21px status pill in the soft ink, and the hairline on the closing panel.
