# Proposal: board-retention-card

## Why

Board DqZaakArchivering titles the archiving card 17px with a 14px muted line, and draws each fact as a 13px label over a
15px value at 500 above a hairline. CnRetentionWidget drew an 18px title and 600 values without hairlines.

## What changes

In the board look CnRetentionWidget's title, subtitle and facts take the board's sizes, weights and hairlines. The 23px
inset comes from the board tab panel's padding (board-tab-panel-padding).
