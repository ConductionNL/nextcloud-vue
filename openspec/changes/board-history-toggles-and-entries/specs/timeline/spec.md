## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The history checkboxes and entries read as the board

A timeline entry SHALL carry its message before its head in the markup, and each look SHALL keep its own visual order. In the board look, the history checkboxes SHALL be a plain box with 14px/400 text 8px after it.

#### Scenario: case history
- **THEN** "Ook wie de zaak las" is a 23px row, and a screen reader hears "Document beoordeeld …" before "Documenten · Lars de Koning · 6 okt"
