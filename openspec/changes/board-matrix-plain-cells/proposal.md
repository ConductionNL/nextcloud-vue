# Proposal: board-matrix-plain-cells

## Why

COMPDIFF r24 on DqProcesanalyse "Dagen per fase": the board draws a matrix cell without a figure ("geen") on the page
ground in the muted ink, so the eye passes over it. Live drew it with no ground in the main ink, as loud as a figure.

## What changes

In the board look, a matrix cell that carries no tone (a text cell without a tone or colour, or an empty cell) takes the
content surface as its ground and the muted ink. Toned cells and the Nextcloud look are unchanged.
