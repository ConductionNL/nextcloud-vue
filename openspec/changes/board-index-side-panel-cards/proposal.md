# Proposal: board-index-side-panel-cards

## Why

On DqTeamwachtrij the cards beside the queue ("Team vandaag", "Werkvoorraad per zaaktype") drew the Nextcloud panel: a
14px dark title on a 12px 16px header over a rule, the body 33px and 39px below the title against the board's 14 and
12, and 16px between the cards against 20. The board draws them as side cards: a 15px muted title on the card's
20px 22px inset, no rule, the body 12px below. The "Werkvoorraad" table drew a 600-weight header and a rule under every
row where the board draws a compact grid with a 13px regular header and rows 8px apart.

## What changes

In the board look a side-panel card on an index page is the board's side card, and a record list drawn as a table is
the board's compact grid. The Nextcloud look is unchanged.
