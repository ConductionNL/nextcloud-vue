## Purpose

A list card's Add is the size of the other title-row buttons.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board list card's Add is the title row's 34px

In the board look, a list card's Add in its title row SHALL take the title row's button size (34px), not a size of its
own.

#### Scenario: DqZaaktypeStatussen

- **WHEN** an administrator opens the Statussen tab
- **THEN** "Documenttype toevoegen" is 34px high and the card title sits as the board draws it
