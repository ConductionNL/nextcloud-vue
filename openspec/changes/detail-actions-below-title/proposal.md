# A detail page can put its header buttons under the title

## Why
Board PqLead draws the lead's header buttons ("Contact vastleggen", the two close buttons, "Bewerken", "Meer") on a row under the title. The board look keeps them on the title row, as DqZaak draws them.

## What changes
With `config.headerActionsBelow: true`, the board header gives the title the whole first row and starts the buttons on a row of their own, from the left. Without it, nothing changes.
