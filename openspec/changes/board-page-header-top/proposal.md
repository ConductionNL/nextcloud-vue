# Proposal: board-page-header-top

## Why

The boards start a page's header 4px under the content's top, with the title at the top of the title row beside the
buttons: the title at 96 and the line under it at 142 (DqZaken, DqMijnWerk, DqZaak; COMPDIFF F010 on 31 boards, F006
on 35). Live the header started at the content's top and the title was centred in the 40px row of buttons, at 92 to
95.2, with the line under it at 138.

## What changes

In the board look the index, detail and dashboard headers carry a 4px top inset, and the index and dashboard titles sit
at the top of their row. Measured live with the rules applied: index and dashboard title at 96, the line under it at
142, the buttons at 96; the detail title at 96.
