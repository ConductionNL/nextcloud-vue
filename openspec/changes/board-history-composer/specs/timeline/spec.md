## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The history composer and entries read as the board

In the board look, the note field SHALL be three lines high, the composer's add button SHALL be the board control height, the title-row buttons SHALL sit 12px apart, and an entry visible to the requester SHALL carry a 12px/600 teal pill.
