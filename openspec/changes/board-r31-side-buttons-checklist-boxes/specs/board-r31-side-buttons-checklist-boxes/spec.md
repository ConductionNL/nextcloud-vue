# board-r31-side-buttons-checklist-boxes Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A side card's button spans the card with its label at the start

In the board look, a facts action in a side card SHALL span the card at the 34px control height. A side card's buttons
(facts actions, the attention flag) SHALL start their label at the button's start.

#### Scenario: DqSociaalDomein

- **THEN** "Toestemming intrekken" is 254 × 34px, and "Markeer voor aandacht" starts its icon and label at the left

### Requirement: A board checklist box is 18px and grey while open

In the board look, a checklist box SHALL be 18px, grey while open and in the primary colour once ticked.

#### Scenario: DqTaak

- **THEN** "Uitnodiging naar de indiener" shows an 18px grey box
