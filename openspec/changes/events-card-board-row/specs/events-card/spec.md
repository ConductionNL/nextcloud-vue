# events-card Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: An appointment row names who is invited and where they stand

An appointment on one day SHALL read as the short date with its hours ("21 okt, 14:00 tot 15:00"). The row SHALL name its invitees after the place, and SHALL end in the pill of the event's status, or of where its invitees stand.

#### Scenario: DqTaak Afspraken

- **GIVEN** the hearing with Fatima El-Amrani invited
- **THEN** the row reads "… 14:00 tot 15:00 · Stadskantoor, zaal 2.04 · Fatima El-Amrani" and ends in "Uitgenodigd"
