## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The manage timeline's text reads as the board draws it

In the board look the manage timeline's subtitle and event count SHALL be 14px muted under any theme, the count line
SHALL stand under a hairline, and the note form SHALL label its template picker and its note above their fields.

- **WHEN** the Historie tab opens in the board look
- **THEN** "Wat er gebeurde, ..." is 14px muted, the count line has a hairline above it, and "Sjabloon" stands above its picker
