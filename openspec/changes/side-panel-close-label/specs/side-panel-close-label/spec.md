## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: the side panel's close button is named "Close side panel"

The close button of CnIndexSidebar and CnObjectSidebar MUST carry the name "Close side panel" (nl "Zijpaneel sluiten") as its aria-label and title.

#### Scenario: the case list's side panel
- GIVEN the side panel of an index page is open
- THEN its close button is named "Close side panel"
