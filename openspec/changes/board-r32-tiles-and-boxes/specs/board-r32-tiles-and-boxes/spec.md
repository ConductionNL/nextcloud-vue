# board-r32-tiles-and-boxes Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board checklist box is drawn as the native checkbox

In the board look, a checklist box SHALL be an 18px square with a 2px corner: a grey 1px edge on white while open, and the
primary colour with a white tick once checked.

#### Scenario: DqTaak

- **THEN** "Datum met de bezwaarcommissie afstemmen" shows a filled blue box with a white tick

### Requirement: A main contact's initials are in the soft ink

In the board look, a main contact's initials SHALL be 16px in the soft ink.

#### Scenario: DqOrganisatie Hoofdcontact

- **THEN** "MV" is 16px in #3d4047
