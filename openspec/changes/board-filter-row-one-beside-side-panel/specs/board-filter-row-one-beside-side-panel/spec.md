## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Filter stays on row 1 beside the side panel

In the board look, while the side panel is open, the Filter button SHALL stay on row 1 after Save view and the view switch SHALL wrap on its own.

#### Scenario: Cases with the side panel open
- GIVEN three chips, Save view, Filter and the view switch in a 760px column
- THEN Filter ends row 1 and the switch starts row 2
