# Proposal: board-dashboard-list-density

## Why

The board look padded a dashboard card's record-list rows 14px top and bottom, following the pipelinq dashboard board.
The Dq dashboards (DqDashboard, DqMijnTeam, DqMijnWerk) pad them 12px, with the title at 600. Where Dq and other
boards differ, Dq wins.

## What changes

In the board look a dashboard record-list row is padded 12px above and below; the title stays at 600. The Nextcloud
look is unchanged.
