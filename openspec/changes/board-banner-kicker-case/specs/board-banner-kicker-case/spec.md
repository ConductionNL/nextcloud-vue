# board-banner-kicker-case Delta

**Status**: proposed
**Scope**: nextcloud-vue

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board dashboard banner's kicker is in sentence case

In the board look, on a dashboard page, a banner's kicker SHALL be 13px, weight 700, without letter spacing and
without a text transform. The plain look SHALL keep its spaced capitals.

#### Scenario: DqMijnWerk Vandaag eerst

- **THEN** the kicker reads "Vandaag eerst", 13px bold, not "VANDAAG EERST"
