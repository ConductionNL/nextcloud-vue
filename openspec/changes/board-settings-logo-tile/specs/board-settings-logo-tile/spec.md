## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board settings logo is a 56px tile

In the board look a CnSettingsField image tile SHALL be 56px square with a 10px radius and no edge, 16px before its
upload button.

- **GIVEN** the Huisstijl card in the board look
- **THEN** the logo tile is 56 by 56 and "Ander logo" starts 16px after it
