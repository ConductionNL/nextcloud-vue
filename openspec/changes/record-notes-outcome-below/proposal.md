# Proposal: record-notes-outcome-below

Board PqContactmoment draws the outcome select and "Uitkomst noteren" on their own line under the save status, at the start of the card. CnRecordNotesWidget put the status and the outcome on one line, the outcome pushed to the end, so the board's toolbar read as missing and live's as extra. `content.outcomeBelow: true` stacks them. Opt-in; no schema change.
