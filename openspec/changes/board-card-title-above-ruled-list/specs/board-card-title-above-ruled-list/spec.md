# board-card-title-above-ruled-list Delta

**Status**: proposed
**Scope**: nextcloud-vue

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A ruled list starts 6px under the card title

In the board look, a bordered dashboard card whose content is a list that rules above its first row (a record list with `ruleAbove`, a task list, a decision list) SHALL put that rule 6px under the title.

#### Scenario: DqMijnTeam Vastgelopen zaken

- **THEN** the rule above the first stalled case stands 6px under the card title
