# board-facts-columns-plain Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Board two-column facts are plain label-over-value pairs

In the board look, two-column facts SHALL show each label 2px above its value. The value SHALL be in regular weight, and
there SHALL be no hairline under a fact.

#### Scenario: DqContact Contact

- **GIVEN** the person Sanne de Vries
- **THEN** "Naam" sits 2px above "Sanne de Vries" in regular weight, with no rule under it
