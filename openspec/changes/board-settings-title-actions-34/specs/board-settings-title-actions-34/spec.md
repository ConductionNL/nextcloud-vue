## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: a board settings card's title-row actions are 34px

In the board look, a labelled button in a settings card's title row MUST be 34px high, and a labelled button in the card's body MUST stay 40px.

#### Scenario: DqVthInstellingen fee rules
- GIVEN the fee rules card with "Regel toevoegen" in its title row
- THEN the button is 34px high
