# Proposal: sidebar-close-label-board

## Why

Boards DqZakenSorteren and DqFilterInMenu name the index sidebar's close button "Zijpaneel sluiten". Nextcloud's
NcAppSidebar says "Zijbalk sluiten" and offers no prop to change it.

## What changes

On the board look CnIndexSidebar sets the sidebar's close label to "Close side panel" ("Zijpaneel sluiten"); the
Nextcloud look keeps Nextcloud's label.
