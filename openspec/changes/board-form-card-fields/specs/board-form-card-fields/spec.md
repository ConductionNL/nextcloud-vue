## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board form card can take the settings card's fields

In the board look a container with `cn-board-form` SHALL draw its Nextcloud text fields, text areas and selects with
the label 14/600 above a 40px control and its labelled buttons 40px, as a board settings card does.
