# Proposal: board-open-actions-toggle-white

## Why

Measured on :8080 (DQ-L8, 23 Oct): DqZaaktypen draws its header "Acties" open, its menu below it, on the main white
ground; live the open trigger read rgb(240, 241, 243), the hover ground, because the pointer that opened it still
rests on it.

## What changes

In the board look, the hover ground of a secondary Actions menu trigger applies only while its menu is closed.

## Reach

Every secondary NcActions trigger in the board look. No prop or schema change.
