# Proposal: board-conversation-lines-as-drawn

## Why

DqZaakContact sets a message's subject line and its note at the normal line height. In the board look they took the
thread's 1.5 (22.5 and 19.5 against 18 and 15.6), so the bubbles with a subject or note stood 4.5 to 9px taller and
pushed the contact log down (COMPDIFF r35: message cards h 98 vs 93.5, 120.5 vs 116, 149 vs 140).

## What changes

- In the board look a message's subject and note sit at the normal line height. The message text keeps 1.5.
