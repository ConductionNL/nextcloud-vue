# A record list avatar can carry the primary tint

## Why
DqZaakGerelateerd draws each related case with its type's initials on the primary tint (#eaf0f7, ink #234a78). r31 drew a grey avatar with the title's initials.

## What changes
`avatar.tone: "primary"` tints the avatar circle with the primary light colour and its light-text ink. Without a tone nothing changes.
