# board-side-person-rows Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board side row with initials keeps them beside the text

In the board look, a side list row with initials SHALL keep the 28px circle on the same line as its text, 8px before it,
and the text SHALL be in regular weight.

#### Scenario: DqSociaalDomein Wie

- **GIVEN** the case "Melding Jeugdwet gezin Oosthaven" with Fatma Kaya and Pieter Jansen on it
- **THEN** each row reads the initials circle, then the person and their role, on one line
