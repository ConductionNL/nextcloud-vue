### Requirement: A board person chip row is 66px

In the board look, a table cell holding a grey person chip with a second line SHALL have 14px of padding above and below.
The name SHALL sit 2px above the second line, which SHALL be 14px on the normal line height.

- **THEN** each row is 66px tall
