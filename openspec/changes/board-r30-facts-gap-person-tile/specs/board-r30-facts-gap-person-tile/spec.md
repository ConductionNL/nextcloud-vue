# board-r30-facts-gap-person-tile Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Board two-column facts start 14px under the title

In the board look, a page card whose facts open on the two-column grid SHALL start its first label 14px under the card
title.

#### Scenario: DqContact Contact

- **GIVEN** Sanne de Vries
- **THEN** "Naam" starts 14px under "Contact"

### Requirement: A board side person row draws its initials on the person-chip tint

In the board look, a side list row's initials circle SHALL use the light primary tint in the primary ink.

#### Scenario: DqSociaalDomein Wie

- **THEN** "PJ" sits on the light primary tint
