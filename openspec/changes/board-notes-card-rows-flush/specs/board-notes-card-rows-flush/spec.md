## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A note in a page card starts at the card's inset

In the board look, a note thread in a page card SHALL draw its icon at the card's content inset and its text 12px after the icon.

#### Scenario: the E-mail tab notes
- GIVEN the Notities card
- THEN the first note's icon starts at the card's 22px inset and its text 12px after it
