# board-sections-chart-single-inset Delta

**Status**: proposed
**Scope**: nextcloud-vue

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A chart inside a dashboard sections card is inset once

In the board look, a chart (HTML body, plot canvas and footnote) inside a section of a sections widget on a dashboard card SHALL take no padding of its own; the section's padding is its inset.

#### Scenario: DqDoorlooptijd Naleving

- **THEN** Naleving per zaaktype, Verdeling doorlooptijd, Maandelijkse SLA-trend and Doorstroom start 17px inside their section, level with the section title
