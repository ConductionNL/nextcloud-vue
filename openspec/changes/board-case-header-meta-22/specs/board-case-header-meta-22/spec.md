## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A case header puts its meta 22px under the title row

In the board look, a detail header with a header widget SHALL put its second row 22px under the title row. A header without one SHALL keep 6px.

#### Scenario: a case page
- GIVEN the board look and a case with its stage bar
- THEN the pills and breadcrumb start 22px under the title row
