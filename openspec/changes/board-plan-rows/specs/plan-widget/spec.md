## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A plan reads as the board's ruled rows

A plan row SHALL show its item's description under the name when it has one. In the board look the rows SHALL be ruled, the name 15px/500, and the state a 13px/600 pill toned by state.

#### Scenario: the case plan
- **THEN** "Mijlpaal: verzoek compleet" reads at weight 500 over "bereikt op 4 okt", with a green "Bereikt" pill
