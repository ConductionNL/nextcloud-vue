## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: a board KPI tile with its badge at the end is padded and spaced as the board draws it

A board-look stacked KPI tile whose title carries its badge at the end MUST be padded 20px 22px, MUST put 8px between its lines and MUST set its figure at the font's normal line height.

#### Scenario: DqDashboard's KPI row
- GIVEN the five KPI tiles of DqDashboard, one of them with a two-line caption
- THEN the tiles are 153px tall
