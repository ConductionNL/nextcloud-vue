# board-sections-card-heading

## Why

A sections widget stacked as cards (a portal's settings tab) titled each card at 18px weight 600 on a 1.5 line. The
dossiq settings boards (DqBeheerinstellingen) draw a settings card title at 17px weight 700 on a 1.3 line, and the Pt
boards follow them (design-system #349). pq3 flagged size, weight and line height on every PtPortalInstellingen card.

## What changes

- In the board look a stacked section card's heading is 17px at weight 700 on a 1.3 line, the dossiq value. The grid variant (a card of cards)
  keeps its 15px 700 heading.
