# board-sections-card-heading

## Why

A sections widget stacked as cards (a portal's settings tab) titled each card at 18px weight 600 on a 1.5 line. The
PtPortalInstellingen board draws the card title at 18px weight 700 on a 1.2 line ("Gegevens van de portal", "Huisstijl",
"Domeinen"), and pq3 flagged weight and line height on every card.

## What changes

- In the board look a stacked section card's heading is weight 700 on a 1.2 line. The grid variant (a card of cards)
  keeps its 15px 700 heading.
