## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A record card's title and meta line sit 10px apart

In the board look a record card's title, meta line and note SHALL read at their normal leading, also under a theme that sets paragraph leading, so the meta line's text starts 10px under the title's text.

#### Scenario: DqAanMijToegewezen

- GIVEN a card titled "Parkeervergunningen binnenstad" over "Woo-verzoek · Linda Bakker"
- THEN the meta text starts 10px under the title text
