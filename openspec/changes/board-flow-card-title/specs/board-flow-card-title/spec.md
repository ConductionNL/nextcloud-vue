## Purpose

The flow board's inspector card is titled as a board card.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A board flow card is titled as a board card

In the board look CnFlowBoard's card title SHALL be `--cn-board-heading-size`, weight 700, at line height `normal`.

#### Scenario: DqFlow "Welstand nodig?"

- **GIVEN** a flow in the board look with a step selected
- **THEN** the inspector card's title "Welstand nodig?" is 17px at 700
