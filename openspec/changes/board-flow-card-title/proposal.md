# Proposal: board-flow-card-title

## Why

COMPDIFF r22 measured the flow board's inspector card "Welstand nodig?" (DqFlow) titled at 16px, weight 600 and line
height 24, where the board titles it as every board card: 17px, 700, at the font's normal line height. CnFlowBoard
styles its card title itself.

## What changes

In the board look the flow board's card title is `--cn-board-heading-size`, 700, line height normal. The Nextcloud look
is unchanged.
