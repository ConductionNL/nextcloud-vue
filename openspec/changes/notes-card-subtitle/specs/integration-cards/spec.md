# integration-cards Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: An integration card shows the widget's subtitle

A component-mode integration widget with a `subtitle` SHALL show it as a muted line under its card title. The notes
card SHALL do so.

#### Scenario: DqTaak Notities

- **GIVEN** the task page's notes widget with subtitle "Only for those working on the task"
- **THEN** the Notities card shows "Alleen voor wie aan de taak werkt" under its title
