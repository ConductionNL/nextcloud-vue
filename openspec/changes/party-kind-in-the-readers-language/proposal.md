# Proposal: party-kind-in-the-readers-language

## Why

DqZaakBetrokkenen r36: the Rollen rows read "Verzoeker · Person", "Melder · Organisation", "Adviseur · Colleague" in a Dutch interface. OpenRegister's parties listing labels its built-in kinds in English, and CnObjectPartiesWidget printed the listing's label.

## What changes

The widget names OpenRegister's built-in kinds (person, organisation, colleague, address) in the reader's language, after a kind the record's own source offers and before the listing's label. nl/en gain "Colleague" (Collega).
