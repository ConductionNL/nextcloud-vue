## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A notes card carries its Add in the title row

In the board look, a CnNotesTab inside a page card SHALL place its "Add note" button at the end of the card's title row, and SHALL draw each note thread with a hairline above and 12px 0 padding.

#### Scenario: the E-mail tab notes
- GIVEN the board look and the case page's Notities card
- THEN "Notitie toevoegen" is level with "Notities", at the right of the card
- AND every note thread opens with a hairline
