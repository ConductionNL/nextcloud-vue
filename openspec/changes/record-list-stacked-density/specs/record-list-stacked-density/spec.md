**Status**: proposed
**Scope**: nextcloud-vue

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A record list can stack its rows

With `density: "stacked"` a record list SHALL draw its rows without row padding and without a rule between them,
10px apart, in the plain and the board look.
