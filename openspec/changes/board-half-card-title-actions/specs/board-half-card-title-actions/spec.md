## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A half-width card keeps its head buttons in the title row

In the board look a settings card's head buttons SHALL stay at the end of the title row in a card at least 400px wide
inside, and drop under the title only in a narrower card.

- **THEN** "Checklist toevoegen" stands at the end of the "Inspectiechecklists" title row
