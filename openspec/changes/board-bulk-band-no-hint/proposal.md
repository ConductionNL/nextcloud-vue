# Proposal: board-bulk-band-no-hint

## Why

The index boards draw the bulk band ("Met de geselecteerde zaken") as its lead and its buttons. Live adds a hint line
under the buttons ("Eerst zien wat er verandert, ..."), which makes the band 28px taller than the board. A one-line hint
is not a feature; the decision (coordinator, 22 Oct) is to leave it out in the board look.

## What changes

In the board look the bulk band's hint line is not drawn. The Nextcloud look keeps it.
